Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026 · Last updated: August 26, 2026

This policy explains what data 3dsante (the "Service") collects, why, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. The Service is operated by an independent 3D animation creator ("we", "us"), who is the data controller and can be reached at learningsoft90@gmail.com.

In short: we collect only what is needed to upload your video to your own TikTok account. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, we do not build profiles, and we do not share it with anyone except TikTok itself in order to perform the upload you asked for.

1. Data we collect

a. TikTok account data

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, TikTok gives us:

DataWhy we need it
Open ID and union ID (TikTok's internal identifiers for your account)To direct each upload to the correct account.
Display name, username, and avatar image URLTo show you which account you are about to publish to, so you do not post to the wrong one.
Access token and refresh tokenTo authorise upload requests to TikTok on your behalf.
Creator posting settings and limits (for example maximum caption length, allowed privacy levels, whether commercial content disclosure is required)To build a valid upload request and to show you the correct options before you publish.

b. Content you choose to publish

The video file, caption, and privacy setting you select for an upload. The video is transferred to TikTok for publication and is not retained by us after the upload finishes.

c. Operational data

Technical logs of upload attempts — timestamp, resulting TikTok publish ID, and any error returned by TikTok. These exist so a failed upload can be diagnosed and retried.

d. What we do not collect

  • Your TikTok password. Authentication happens entirely on TikTok's own login page; we never see it.
  • Your TikTok followers, comments, direct messages, or the content of other users.
  • Analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking cookies. This website sets no cookies.
  • Location data, contacts, or any special category of personal data.

2. How we use data

We use the data above only to:

  • authenticate you with TikTok and keep that session valid;
  • publish the videos you explicitly choose to publish, to the account you chose;
  • show you the correct posting options and confirm the target account;
  • diagnose and retry failed uploads;
  • comply with legal obligations and with TikTok's developer requirements.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, model training, or any purpose unrelated to publishing your own content.

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we process your data on the basis of performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) — the data is necessary to deliver the publishing service you asked for — and, for the connection of your TikTok account, your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you give on TikTok's consent screen and may withdraw at any time.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. Data leaves the Service only in these cases:

  • TikTok — we send your video, caption, and privacy setting to TikTok in order to publish it. TikTok's handling of that data is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • Legal requirement — where we are compelled by valid legal process, and only to the extent required.

5. Retention and deletion

DataKept for
Access and refresh tokensUntil you disconnect the app or revoke access in TikTok, or until the token expires — whichever comes first. Then deleted.
Account identifiers and profile dataDeleted within 7 days of disconnection or revocation.
Video files submitted for uploadDeleted as soon as TikTok confirms the upload, and in any case within 24 hours.
Upload logs (timestamp, publish ID, error code)Up to 90 days, then deleted.

If TikTok notifies us that a user has deleted their account or revoked access, we delete the corresponding data without further request.

6. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • Revoke access — in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. This immediately stops the Service from publishing.
  • Request access to the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict processing.

Email learningsoft90@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or the UK you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

7. Security

All communication with TikTok uses HTTPS/TLS. Access tokens are stored encrypted at rest and are never exposed in the user interface, in logs, or in URLs. Access to stored data is limited to the operator. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and any required authority without undue delay.

8. International transfers

The Service is operated from the Republic of Korea. TikTok processes data in the regions described in its own privacy policy. Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA or the UK, it is protected by the safeguards described in TikTok's privacy policy and by appropriate contractual measures on our side.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children. It is intended only for adults (18 or older) who publish their own creative work. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that we have, we delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. For material changes we will give notice through the Service before the change takes effect.
